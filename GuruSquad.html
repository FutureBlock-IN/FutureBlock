Common Questions

Straight answers.

We have data in many systems — can you unify it?+
That's our core competency. We build data pipelines that ingest from ERP, CRM, e-commerce, spreadsheets, and APIs into a central warehouse. Your dashboards then pull from one source of truth instead of ten disconnected exports.
Power BI or Metabase — which should we use?+
Power BI is ideal for Microsoft-centric organizations needing enterprise governance. Metabase excels at self-serve analytics with lower licensing costs. We evaluate your team skills, data volume, and budget before recommending — and we're proficient in both.
How long before we see our first dashboard?+
We deliver a working prototype dashboard within 48 hours of receiving data access. Full production dashboards with automated refresh typically ship within 4–6 weeks, depending on data complexity and number of sources.
Can you build predictive models, not just reports?+
Yes. We build demand forecasting, churn prediction, pricing optimization, and anomaly detection models using Python and deploy them into your existing workflows. Models are monitored for drift and retrained on schedule — not left to decay in a notebook.
Do we need a data warehouse before starting?+
Not necessarily. We can start with direct-source dashboards for quick wins while building the warehouse in parallel. However, for organizations with 3+ data sources, a warehouse pays for itself in reduced maintenance and improved query performance within months.
