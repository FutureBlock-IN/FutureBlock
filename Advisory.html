Common Questions

Straight answers.

How is your Strategic Advisory different from the Big Four?+
We don't sell implementation hours disguised as strategy. Our Strategic Advisory engagements are fixed-scope with clear deliverables — audit reports, roadmaps, and vendor scorecards you own outright. No recurring retainer required to access the insights.
Do you recommend specific vendors?+
We evaluate vendors against your criteria using structured scoring matrices. We have no reseller agreements that bias our recommendations. If we suggest a platform, it's because it fits your stack, team, and budget — not because we earn a commission.
How long does a typical technology audit take?+
Most audits complete in 2–3 weeks depending on organization size. We conduct stakeholder interviews, system inventory, and process mapping in parallel. You receive a draft findings report within 10 business days.
Can you advise without implementing?+
Absolutely. Many clients engage us purely for Strategic Advisory — roadmap development, vendor selection, and architecture review — then execute internally or with another partner. We provide enough detail for any competent team to implement.
What industries do you have Strategic Advisory experience in?+
Banking, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, and logistics are our strongest verticals. Our consultants have led technology strategy at Fortune 500 scale across North America, Europe, and the Middle East before founding FutureBlock.
