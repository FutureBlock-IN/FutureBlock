Common Questions

Straight answers.

AWS, Azure, or GCP — which cloud should we choose?+
It depends on your existing investments, compliance requirements, and workload characteristics. We assess each platform against your specific needs and can design multi-cloud strategies when vendor lock-in is a concern. We hold certifications across all three major providers.
Can you migrate without downtime?+
For most workloads, yes. We use blue-green deployments, database replication, and DNS cutover strategies to achieve zero-downtime migrations. Every migration plan includes a tested rollback procedure in case issues arise during cutover.
How do you control cloud costs?+
We implement tagging policies, right-sizing reviews, reserved instance planning, and automated shutdown schedules for non-production environments. Our managed operations clients typically see 35% cost reduction within the first 6 months through continuous optimization.
Do you handle security and compliance?+
Security is built into every architecture — IAM policies, encryption at rest and in transit, VPC segmentation, and audit logging. We help clients meet SOC 2, ISO 27001, and industry-specific compliance requirements with documented controls and evidence collection.
What does managed cloud operations include?+
24/7 monitoring, incident response with defined SLAs, patch management, backup verification, cost reporting, and monthly optimization reviews. You get a dedicated operations team without hiring in-house DevOps engineers.

How much is your current infrastructure costing you?

We'll audit your cloud spend and show you where you're overpaying — at no cost.
