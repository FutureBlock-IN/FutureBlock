Common Questions

Straight answers.

We've done AI POCs that went nowhere — how are you different?+
We scope every AI engagement with a production deployment target from day one. POCs are time-boxed to 2–3 weeks with explicit go/no-go criteria. If it works, we integrate it into your existing systems immediately — not hand you a Jupyter notebook and walk away.
Is our data safe with LLM providers?+
We assess data sensitivity before selecting models. For confidential data, we deploy private instances, use on-premise open-source models, or implement RAG architectures that keep your data in your infrastructure. We never send sensitive data to public APIs without explicit client approval.
What AI use cases deliver the fastest ROI?+
Document processing, customer support automation, and internal knowledge search typically show ROI within 8–12 weeks. We run a use case prioritization workshop to rank opportunities by commercial impact, data readiness, and implementation complexity before recommending where to start.
Do you build custom models or use off-the-shelf?+
Both, depending on the use case. We use foundation models (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini) with RAG for most knowledge applications. Custom fine-tuning and classification models are built when domain-specific accuracy requirements exceed what prompting alone can achieve.
How do you handle AI governance and compliance?+
Every production AI system includes model monitoring, output logging, bias testing, and explainability documentation. We build governance frameworks aligned with emerging EU AI Act requirements and industry-specific regulations — so you're audit-ready as regulation matures.
