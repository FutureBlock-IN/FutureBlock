Common Questions

Straight answers.

Which processes are good candidates for automation?+
High-volume, rule-based tasks with structured inputs — invoice processing, data reconciliation, report generation, and approval workflows. We run a discovery workshop to map candidates and rank them by ROI before recommending where to start.
UiPath or Power Automate — which do you recommend?+
It depends on your existing Microsoft investment, process complexity, and team skills. Power Automate integrates seamlessly with M365 environments. UiPath excels at complex, cross-application automation. We evaluate both against your criteria and recommend without bias.
Will automation replace our employees?+
Our clients use automation to redirect skilled staff from repetitive work to higher-value activities — customer service, analysis, and strategy. We design automations that augment your team, not eliminate roles without a deliberate workforce plan.
How do you handle exceptions and errors?+
Every automation includes exception handling paths, alerting, and human-in-the-loop workflows for edge cases. We build monitoring dashboards so your team knows immediately when intervention is needed — not days later during reconciliation.
What does ongoing support look like?+
We provide post-deployment monitoring, quarterly optimization reviews, and SLA-backed support for critical automations. As your processes evolve, we update workflows to match — automation is never a one-and-done project.

Tell us your most painful manual process. We'll tell you how to eliminate it.
